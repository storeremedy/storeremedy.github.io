FAQ

Questions store owners ask

What access do you need to my store?

For the audit — none: it runs on the public site. For fixes, a staging copy or admin access with a separate account you can revoke any time. Every change is documented.

What if the audit finds nothing serious?

Then we tell you exactly that, and you've spent $150 to learn your store is healthy — with a report you can keep. We don't invent work; the audit is valuable either way.

My store isn't OpenCart, WooCommerce or Shopify. Can you still help?

Usually yes — speed and SEO problems are similar across platforms. Send the link and we'll tell you honestly whether we're the right fit before you pay anything.

How do payments work?

Invoice with card or bank transfer (Wise/Payoneer), or through Upwork if you prefer its escrow protection. Fixed prices are agreed in writing before work starts.
