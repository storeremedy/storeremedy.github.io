What the fix sprint actually did

  • Images: 5000-pixel, multi-megabyte "camera originals" resized to display dimensions and re-encoded — the hero banner went from 2.9 MB to 80 KB with no visible quality loss.
  • Third-party cleanup: removed nine accumulated render-blocking scripts and stylesheets (duplicate jQuery versions, unused font families, abandoned widgets) — the kind of junk every store collects over the years.
  • Render path: deferred every non-critical script, made font loading asynchronous, and fixed a carousel that kept the hero image invisible for 4 extra seconds after it had already downloaded.
  • Server: enabled gzip compression (HTML went from 25 KB to 4 KB on the wire) and browser caching for static assets.
  • SEO basics: real title tag instead of "Your Store", meta description, missing H1, robots.txt and sitemap.xml.
